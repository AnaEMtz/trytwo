History

Timeline

A selective look at milestones in UN sanctions and in the monitoring of sanctions evasion.

1966

First mandatory sanctions

Resolution 232 imposes selective mandatory sanctions on Southern Rhodesia.

1990

Comprehensive sanctions on Iraq

Resolution 661 follows the invasion of Kuwait, establishing a wide trade and financial embargo.

2000

The Fowler Report

A Panel of Experts report on UNITA sanctions-busting in Angola names states and individuals, becoming a landmark for sanctions monitoring.

2003–2004

Monitoring groups multiply

Expert bodies are created for Somalia (res. 1519) and the Democratic Republic of the Congo (res. 1533).

2006

DPRK sanctions begin

Resolution 1718 follows a nuclear test and creates the sanctions committee.

2009

DPRK Panel of Experts

Resolution 1874 establishes a Panel to support the 1718 Committee.

2011

Libya

Resolution 1970 imposes sanctions and resolution 1973 creates a Panel of Experts.

Reading the timeline. The pattern is consistent: as sanctions expand, so do the evasion methods, and so does the need for independent monitoring.

2016 onwards

Broader sectoral measures, major changes to monitoring and new reporting mechanisms.

2016–2017

DPRK measures expand

Resolutions 2270, 2321, 2371, 2375 and 2397 add bans and caps on key exports, imports, joint ventures and overseas labour.

2016

Iran Panel wound down

Following the nuclear agreement, earlier nuclear-related UN sanctions on Iran are terminated.

2023

Mali Panel ends

A Council vote to renew the sanctions regime is vetoed, ending the Panel’s mandate.

March–April 2024

DPRK Panel mandate ends

A draft resolution to renew the Panel is vetoed on 28 March 2024; its mandate expires on 30 April 2024.

October 2024

Multilateral Sanctions Monitoring Team

Eleven states launch the MSMT to continue monitoring and reporting on DPRK sanctions violations.

2025

First MSMT report

The MSMT publishes its first report, focusing on DPRK–Russia military cooperation.

September 2025

Iran sanctions reimposed

UN sanctions on Iran are reinstated through the “snapback” mechanism of resolution 2231.

This timeline is selective and based on publicly available information. Always verify details against official UN sources, especially for recent developments.
