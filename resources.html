Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to the questions people ask most.

What counts as sanctions evasion?

Any act intended to circumvent measures imposed by the Security Council, such as concealing a sanctioned party’s involvement, disguising the origin of goods or moving funds through intermediaries.

Who appoints Panel of Experts members?

The Secretary-General appoints the experts, generally in consultation with the relevant sanctions committee, and they serve in their individual capacity.

Are Panel reports public?

Reports are submitted to the sanctions committee and the Security Council and are generally published, although some annexes or details may be restricted.

Can a Panel punish violators?

No. Panels investigate and recommend. Designations and penalties are decided by the Security Council or applied by member states through national law.

Why did some Panels end?

Panel mandates require Council renewal. In some cases, such as Mali in 2023 and the DPRK in 2024, a veto prevented renewal.
